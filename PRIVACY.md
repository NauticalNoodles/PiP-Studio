# Privacy policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

PiP Studio doesn't collect, store or send any personal data.

- It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or ads, and it doesn't connect to the internet.
- To show picture-in-picture windows, it uses Windows' own live window thumbnails on your PC. Nothing it shows is recorded or saved, and nothing leaves your device.
- Its settings and the list of PiPs to restore are saved only on your PC, in PiP Studio's settings folder. Uninstalling it (and choosing to delete settings) removes them.
- It reads game controllers and keyboard shortcuts only to control PiP Studio itself.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/NauticalNoodles/PiP-Studio/issues
