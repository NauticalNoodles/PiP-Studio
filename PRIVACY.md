# Privacy policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

PiP Studio doesn't collect, store or send any personal data.

- It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or ads.
- The only time it goes online is to check GitHub for a new version of PiP Studio (once a day, and when you press "Check now") and to download an update when you ask for one. Nothing about you or your PC is sent; like any website, GitHub sees your IP address. You can turn the daily check off in Settings > General > Updates. The Microsoft Store version doesn't do this at all: the Store updates it.
- The phone companion (Settings > General > Phone companion) is off unless you turn it on. Then PiP Studio answers devices on your local network only, and only phones you paired (by typing the PIN the phone shows into PiP Studio) can control it. It sends your phone the names of your open PiPs and of the apps playing sound, and their volume levels (and, only while you have a PiP's Preview turned on, small snapshots of what that PiP shows), over your home network, encrypted with a key only that phone and your PC have. Nothing goes over the internet or to anyone else. Each paired phone's key is stored locked to your Windows account, and Remove forgets it. The Microsoft Store version doesn't include the phone companion.
- To show picture-in-picture windows, it uses Windows' own live window thumbnails on your PC. Nothing it shows is recorded or saved, and nothing leaves your device.
- Its settings and the list of PiPs to restore are saved only on your PC, in PiP Studio's settings folder. Uninstalling it (and choosing to delete settings) removes them.
- It reads game controllers and keyboard shortcuts only to control PiP Studio itself.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/NauticalNoodles/PiP-Studio/issues
